## Overview

Data sovereignty means that data is subject to the laws of the country in which it is processed and stored - and that no foreign government, court, or authority can compel access without the knowledge and consent of the data owner under EU law.

For organisations subject to **NIS2**, **GDPR**, national critical infrastructure regulations, or sector-specific requirements (healthcare, defence, financial services), this is not a preference - it is a requirement.

ROOTKey supports sovereignty at three levels - the standard EU-region cloud, a dedicated deployment on EU-incorporated cloud, and a fully on-premise installation where no component leaves your infrastructure.

## Infrastructure Stack

The choice of infrastructure determines the sovereignty level of the deployment:

| Component | Provider | Sovereignty level | Notes |
|-----------|----------|------------------|-------|
| **Blockchain** | Polygon | Global | Public PoS network - distributed globally; no single jurisdiction controls the network. Used by RKP-1 and RKP-3; RKP-2 uses no blockchain |
| **Cloud (standard)** | Azure / AWS | EU-region available | Microsoft and Amazon are US-incorporated; CLOUD Act exposure remains |
| **Cloud (EU)** | OVH | EU | Dedicated deployment on request, on OVH Managed Kubernetes. French company; no US parent company; EU jurisdiction |
| **On-premise** | Customer infrastructure | Customer's own | The [on-premise installation](/pages/on-premise/overview) runs the platform inside your perimeter, egress-only or fully air-gapped |

## What Changes With Each Level

### Enhanced EU: OVH instead of Azure / AWS

**OVH** is a French cloud provider with no US parent company. A dedicated ROOTKey deployment on OVH Managed Kubernetes means that off-chain data storage, API processing, and platform operations are outside the jurisdiction of US law enforcement and the CLOUD Act. ROOTKey does not run on OVH by default - the deployment is provisioned on request.

### Sovereign EU: your own infrastructure

The [on-premise installation](/pages/on-premise/overview) places the complete ROOTKey Data Plane inside your perimeter. In egress-only mode the only outbound link is to the ROOTKey Control Plane, for licensing and telemetry - never files, users, or metadata. In air-gapped mode there is no link at all.

## Regulatory Frameworks That Require or Benefit From Sovereignty

| Framework | Sovereignty relevance |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **GDPR** | Processing of personal data must be lawful - transfers outside EEA require adequate safeguards; EU-sovereign deployment eliminates transfer risk |
| **NIS2** | Critical entity operators may be subject to national requirements for data sovereignty in sensitive infrastructure |
| **EU Cybersecurity Act** | Certification schemes (EUCS) increasingly require EU-sovereign cloud for high-assurance levels |
| **DORA** | ICT third-party risk - US CLOUD Act exposure is a material risk for financial entities under ECB scrutiny |
| **Healthcare (EU)** | Clinical trial data and patient records processed under EU CTR and GDPR benefit from EU-sovereign infrastructure |

## Requesting an EU Deployment

Both the dedicated OVH deployment and the on-premise installation are available on request for organisations with demonstrated sovereignty requirements. Scoping involves:

1. Choosing the level - dedicated OVH deployment or on-premise
2. Sizing and network design (for on-premise: egress-only or air-gapped)
3. API endpoints scoped to the dedicated deployment
4. SLA and data processing agreements under EU law
