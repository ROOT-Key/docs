<Warning>
  **Roadmap - not available yet.** RKP-3 Sovereign EU depends on deploying a private blockchain inside the customer's infrastructure, which the on-premise installer does not do today. Until then, the highest-sovereignty option for hybrid workloads is [RKP-3 Enhanced EU](/pages/protocols/rkp-3-eu), and the highest-sovereignty option overall is [RKP-2 Sovereign EU](/pages/protocols/rkp-2-sovereign) (on-premise, no blockchain).
</Warning>

This is the planned highest-sovereignty variant of [RKP-3 (Hybrid)](/pages/protocols/rkp-3-hybrid). The routing stays the same - bulk data off-chain, critical events on-chain - but on-chain records land on a private chain operated inside the customer's perimeter instead of the public Polygon network.

## Planned Sovereignty Profile

| Component | Provider | Jurisdiction | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------|
| **Blockchain (on-chain path)** | Private chain, customer-operated | Customer EU facility | Deployed alongside the platform by the on-premise installer |
| **API processing** | Customer on-premise | Customer EU facility | ROOTKey [on-premise installation](/pages/on-premise/overview) |
| **Off-chain storage** | Customer on-premise | Customer EU facility | Data never leaves the organisation's infrastructure |
| **Sovereignty level** | **Full EU** | 100% customer infrastructure | No component outside the customer's control |

## The Complete Sovereignty Matrix - All Protocol Variants

| Protocol | Blockchain | Infrastructure | Sovereignty | Availability |

| **RKP-3 Sovereign EU** | Private chain | On-premise | Full EU | Roadmap |

<Note>
  A private chain moves the trust boundary: verification is no longer possible from a public block explorer by any third party, only by parties given access to the chain. This is the trade-off for keeping every component under the customer's jurisdiction.
</Note>
