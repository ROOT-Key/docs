<Warning>
  **Roadmap - not available yet.** RKP-1 Sovereign EU depends on deploying a private blockchain inside the customer's infrastructure, which the on-premise installer does not do today. Until then, the highest-sovereignty option for on-chain anchoring is [RKP-1 Enhanced EU](/pages/protocols/rkp-1-eu), and [RKP-2 Sovereign EU](/pages/protocols/rkp-2-sovereign) (on-premise, no blockchain) is in development.
</Warning>

This is the planned highest-sovereignty variant of [RKP-1 (Full On-Chain)](/pages/protocols/rkp-1-on-chain). The anchoring mechanics stay the same - every operation is written on-chain - but the blockchain becomes a private chain operated inside the customer's perimeter instead of the public Polygon network.

## Planned Sovereignty Profile

| Component | Provider | Jurisdiction | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------|
| **Blockchain** | Private chain, customer-operated | Customer EU facility | Deployed alongside the platform by the on-premise installer |
| **API processing** | Customer on-premise | Customer EU facility | ROOTKey [on-premise installation](/pages/on-premise/overview) |
| **Off-chain storage** | Customer on-premise | Customer EU facility | Data never leaves the organisation's infrastructure |
| **Sovereignty level** | **Full EU** | 100% customer infrastructure | No component outside the customer's control |

---

## How It Will Differ from RKP-1 Standard and RKP-1 Enhanced EU

| Property | RKP-1 Standard | RKP-1 Enhanced EU | RKP-1 Sovereign EU *(roadmap)* |
|----------|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Blockchain | Polygon | Polygon | **Private chain (customer-operated)** |
| Infrastructure | ROOTKey cloud (Azure / AWS) | Dedicated deployment on OVH | **Customer on-premise** |
| Blockchain jurisdiction | Global | Global | **Customer EU facility** |
| Full EU sovereignty | No | No | **Yes** |
| Verification | Public (Polygonscan) | Public (Polygonscan) | **Within the private chain's participants** |
| Availability | Standard | On request | **Roadmap** |

<Note>
  A private chain moves the trust boundary: verification is no longer possible from a public block explorer by any third party, only by parties given access to the chain. This is the trade-off for keeping every component under the customer's jurisdiction.
</Note>
